## 5–12. Standard (service) pages

All share one template: H1 standard name → intro paragraph → "Advantages/Benefits" bullets → "For More Information Contact us" → SEO keyword line. There is no pricing, process, timeline, FAQ or form on the page (except 27001:2022).

### 5. ISO 9001:2015, QMS (`/iso-9001-2015/`)
- Intro: an international QMS framework for consistent operations and customer satisfaction; works best when rolled out organisation-wide.
- Advantages (9): continual improvement, competitive edge, customer satisfaction and international recognition, working structure, brand value, efficiency, productivity and cost savings, communication and morale, sales opportunities.
- 7 Quality Principles (wrongly headed "Advantage…" a second time): Customer focus, Leadership, Engagement of people, Process approach, Improvement, Evidence-based decision making, Relationship management.
- Sub-heads: "Process Approach", "Plan Do Check Act Cycle" (image/diagram).

### 6. ISO 14001:2015, EMS (`/iso-14001-2015/`)
- Intro: helps achieve environmental outcomes and integrates the EMS into strategy.
- Key changes (5): leadership commitment, strategic alignment, proactive environmental protection, communication strategy, life-cycle thinking.
- Advantages (6): regulatory compliance, leadership/employee engagement, reputation, strategic aims, cost efficiency, supplier performance.

### 7. ISO 22000:2018, FSMS (`/iso-22000-2018/`)
- Intro: food-chain safety framework, adopted by BIS as IS/ISO 22000:2018; combines Codex HACCP principles with PRPs; risk analysis at its core.
- Benefits (8): HACCP + PRPs/OPRPs, hazard identification and control, standalone or integrated, suits small organisations, focused FSMS, prevention emphasis, legal compliance, emergency management.

### 8. ISO 27001:2013, ISMS (`/iso-27001-2013/`)
- Intro: published 25 Sep 2013 (replaced 2005 edition); risk-based ISMS; third-party certificate shows controls against unauthorised access.
- Advantages (8): awareness; CIA + authenticity + reliability; business continuity; legal certainty; reduced management liability; cost savings; global market access; proof to customers.
- ⚠️ Outdated: the 2013 edition was withdrawn after the transition deadline (Oct 2025).

### 9. ISO 27001:2022 (`/iso-27001-2022/`), the richest service page
- **Side box "B4Q Provides":** Personal certification (1-day transition course 2013→2022; 5-day Lead Auditor 27001:2022). Company certification (apply for the 2022 standard). **Contact Us Now!** form (Name, Mobile, Email, Message).
- Intro: published 25 Oct 2022, replaces the 2013 edition.
- Control stats: **93 controls: 11 new, 24 merged, 58 updated**. Grouped into **4 themes** (was 14 domains): Organizational 37 · People 8 · Physical 14 · Technological 34.
- **New controls (11):** 5.7 Threat intelligence · 5.23 Cloud services security · 5.30 ICT readiness for BC · 7.4 Physical security monitoring · 8.9 Configuration mgmt · 8.10 Information deletion · 8.11 Data masking · 8.12 DLP · 8.16 Monitoring · 8.22 Web filtering · 8.28 Secure coding.
- **Consolidated controls (19)**, each mapped to 27002:2013 clauses (e.g., 5.1 Policies ← 5.1.1/5.1.2; 5.15 Access control ← 9.1.1/9.1.2; 8.32 Change mgmt ← 12.1.2, 14.2.2–14.2.4).
- Advantages (7): all information forms, cyber resilience, central framework, organisation-wide protection, evolving threats, lower spend, CIA.
- ⚠️ The copy reads as machine-spun ("info security", "commonplace", "associate structure approach"). It needs a full rewrite.

### 10. ISO 27701:2019, PIMS (`/iso-27701-2019/`)
- Intro: privacy extension to ISO 27001/27002 (published 6 Aug 2019).
- Structure: Clause 5 (27001 reqs) · 6 (27002 reqs) · 7 (PII Controllers) · 8 (PII Processors). Annex A–F (controller and processor controls; mappings to ISO 29100, **GDPR**, ISO 27018/29151; applying to 27001/27002).
- Two routes: 27001 first, then 27701; or both as a single project.

### 11. ISO 45001:2018, OH&S (`/iso-450012018/`)
- Intro: an OH&S management system to prevent injury and ill health. Intended outcomes: (a) continual improvement, (b) legal compliance, (c) OH&S objectives.
- Advantages (6): ⚠️ copy-pasted from ISO 14001 (it even mentions "environmental issues"). Needs rewriting.

### 12. ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018, ITSMS (`/iso-iec-20000-1-2018/`)
- Four prose paragraphs, no bullets: SMS basis aligned to Annex SL; covers the service lifecycle (plan, design, transition, deliver, improve); lists potential users (customers, providers, assessors, trainers); now applied beyond IT; changes since the 2011 edition.
